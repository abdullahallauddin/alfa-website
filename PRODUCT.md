# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Prospective clients, partners, and talent in heavy industry — oil & gas, marine & offshore, construction, defense, renewable energy — primarily across Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf. They arrive evaluating whether ALFA Group is a credible, capable, one-stop contracting partner. Context: desktop and mobile, often a procurement or business-development lead doing due diligence.

## Product Purpose
The ALFA Group corporate marketing site. It exists to establish credibility and breadth — communicating that ALFA is a serious multi-division industrial group (Marine & Offshore, Construction, Fabrication, Trading, ICT, Facility Management, Joinery) with a proven track record. Success = a visitor leaves confident enough to make contact or shortlist ALFA.

## Brand Personality
Confident, disciplined, engineering-grade. Three words: **solid, precise, trustworthy**. The voice is corporate but not cold — it should feel like a well-run EPC group, not a startup. Emotional goal: reassurance and competence.

## Anti-references
- Startup/SaaS landing pages (gradient-text, pastel, playful illustration).
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif + italic drop caps + ruled three-column kicker grids) — wrong register for an industrial group.
- Identical icon-card grids repeated down the page.
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section.

## Design Principles
1. **Earned confidence** — show scale and capability (divisions, stats, presence) plainly; let the work speak, don't oversell.
2. **One idea per fold** — the home page is a section-locked scroll; each viewport carries a single clear message.
3. **Cohesive palette, distinct compositions** — every section shares the deep-navy identity but earns its own layout.
4. **Legibility first** — light type on deep navy with real contrast; restraint with the accent blue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body/large text must meet WCAG AA contrast on the navy ground. All motion has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Section-snap must not trap keyboard or screen-reader users (content remains in normal document flow).
